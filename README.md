## What Is a Single Vendor E-commerce Platform?

A single-vendor e-commerce platform is an online shopping system where one business or seller manages the complete store.

Unlike a multi-vendor marketplace, where multiple independent sellers manage their own products, a single-vendor platform gives one business centralized control over:

- Product catalog and categories
- Pricing and discounts
- Inventory and stock
- Customer accounts
- Shopping carts and orders
- Payments and transactions
- Shipping and delivery
- Sales reports and business operations

It is suitable for businesses that want to sell products directly to customers through their own branded website.

## Single Vendor vs Multi-Vendor E-commerce

| Feature | Single Vendor | Multi-Vendor |
|---|---|---|
| Store ownership | One business | Multiple sellers |
| Product management | Centralized | Seller-based |
| Inventory control | Centralized | Seller-managed or centralized |
| Order management | One business | Marketplace and sellers |
| Seller dashboards | Not required | Required |
| Commission management | Usually not required | Often required |
| Best suited for | Brands and retailers | Online marketplaces |

If you want to build a marketplace where multiple sellers can register and sell products, a custom multi-vendor e-commerce solution may be more suitable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a single-vendor e-commerce website?

It is an online store managed by one business that sells its own products directly to customers through a centralized platform.

### How is a single-vendor platform different from a marketplace?

A single-vendor platform has one store owner managing products and orders. A marketplace allows multiple independent sellers to list products and manage their sales.

### Can I manage products and inventory myself?

Yes. An administration dashboard can be developed to manage products, categories, stock, pricing, and orders.

### Can you integrate payment gateways?

Yes. We can integrate suitable payment gateways based on your business location, payment provider, and project requirements.

### Can the platform support wholesale and retail customers?

Yes. B2B and B2C purchasing workflows, customer groups, and different pricing structures can be included when required.

### Can I add a mobile application later?

Yes. The platform can be planned with APIs that support future Android and iOS application development.
